What this is

The picture of M seen from the vertex d: the thick edges are the link of d, the ten coloured tetrahedra are the ones that do not touch d, and the dashed rays are the edges going to d, which is at infinity.

στ = (a g c)(b e h) keeps d and f. The picture is drawn so that στ is a rotation by a third of a turn about the dotted axis, which passes through f and the centre of the triangle beh. The grey lines are the link of d before the motion.

The coordinates were found by computer search for this page and checked numerically, not exactly: the twenty tetrahedra have the same orientation, and a ray from the origin meets one triangle of the link of d.

The three symmetries of order two (σ, τ, τστ) are not shown: no picture of this kind makes one of them a rotation (argument by Claude, not reviewed).

Source for σ, τ and the list of tetrahedra: v4, Section 1.
