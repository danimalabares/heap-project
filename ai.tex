\section{The AI model landscape}

Snapshot checked on 29 October 2026.
This section concerns general-purpose
language and multimodal models, rather
than every kind of AI.

GPT and Claude are not the only powerful
families. Gemini is a direct frontier
competitor; Chinese laboratories also
produce capable reasoning and coding
models. There is no single winner for
every task, budget, or deployment.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Model, application, and agent.}
A model generates outputs from inputs.
A chat application adds interfaces,
memory, file handling, and subscriptions.
An agent adds tools and a loop for
carrying out actions. Thus, comparing
ChatGPT with Claude Code mixes levels:
success can depend on the tools and
instructions as much as on the model.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Families beyond GPT and Claude.}
The following is a map, not a ranking.
Release names are dated examples.

\begin{itemize}
\item Google DeepMind: Gemini.
Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon are current
examples. Gemini is a serious alternative
for reasoning, coding, and work involving
different kinds of input. Google also
offers Gemma as a separate open-model
family; Gemini and Gemma are not synonyms.
See Google's model catalogue [1].

\item DeepSeek.
A Chinese laboratory with downloadable
models and hosted services. DeepSeek
V4.1 Flash 1017 is a current example
listed by the hosting provider Mistral.
Reasoning, coding, and cost efficiency
make this family worth comparing [2,3].

\item Alibaba: Qwen.
A broad family, rather than one chatbot.
Qwen3.8-Max targets coding and extended
workflows; other variants target
multimodal tasks. The August announcement
promised Max weights for the following
week: an announcement alone does not
verify that a particular download is
available under a particular licence [4].

\item Moonshot AI: Kimi.
Kimi K3.1 Pro was announced on 23 October.
The developer describes an open-weight
model with a million-token context and
improved coding capabilities [5].
A large context limit measures input
capacity, not reliable recall of every
detail or correctness of reasoning.

\item Z.ai: GLM.
GLM-5.3 and GLM-5.3 Flash are further
Chinese alternatives. Mistral lists them
as third-party models with MIT licences;
Flash targets visual coding and agent
workflows [2]. A hosting platform can
serve another laboratory's model.

\item Grok, from the xAI ecosystem.
Grok 4.7 offers configurable reasoning,
coding, and tool use. Its documentation
explicitly distinguishes the model from
web and X search: current information
requires those tools to be enabled [6].

\item Mistral, based in France.
Its catalogue includes general-purpose
models, smaller models, document OCR,
and audio models. Examples include
Mistral Large 4 and Small 4 [2].
This is especially relevant when model
licensing and deployment control matter.

\item Meta and other developers.
Meta's Llama and Muse families also
belong on the map; the current independent
comparison includes Muse Wonder [7].
The examples above are not an exhaustive
list of capable laboratories.
\end{itemize}

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{How powerful are they?}
On the Artificial Analysis leaderboard
checked for this snapshot, Gemini 4 Carbon
(high) scores 59, Claude Opus 5.5
(max with fallback) 58, and GPT-6 Astra
(max) 57 on its Intelligence Index.
Kimi K3.1 Pro scores 46 [7].
These are index scores, not percentages
of mathematical ability. They measure
particular versions and configurations,
not whole companies or chat products.
A different evaluation can change the
ordering.

There is also evidence of competitive
cost without equal frontier capability.
NIST's May 2026 evaluation found DeepSeek
V4 Pro cheaper than GPT-5.4 mini on five
of seven cost comparisons, but behind
the leading US models on its overall
capability evaluation [3].
This is evidence about that dated test,
not a verdict on every later DeepSeek.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Open weights and local use.}
Open weights let users obtain the trained
parameters, subject to the licence.
They do not automatically provide the
training data or complete reproducible
training procedure [8].
Nor does downloadable mean laptop-sized.
Running a model locally depends on its
size, numerical precision, memory needs,
and software compatibility.
For the existing Monterey Mac, check
those requirements before selecting any
local model or runtime.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{A useful experiment for our work.}
My recommendation is to try Gemini as a
third opinion beside GPT and Claude, then
one of Kimi or DeepSeek on the same small
set of mathematical and coding tasks.
Keep the source material and instructions
fixed. Compare correctness, missing
hypotheses, invented citations, time,
and cost. Include a task whose answer
is already known and a proof with a
deliberate gap. Agreement between models
is not independent mathematical evidence:
their errors may be correlated.
A convincing answer or benchmark score
does not establish a proof.
