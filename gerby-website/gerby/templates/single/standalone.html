Spanish. Unfinished. 67 pages. TeX source; the one figure (Homología/H1.png) was recovered from the old git history.

Course notes (20 lectures plus a summary for the first test), course by Vinicius Ramos; the notes do not name the institution or term (files dated January–February 2025): manifolds, Sard’s theorem, Whitney topologies and transversality, jets and multijets, intersection theory, degree and index, Poincaré–Hopf and Hopf theorems, Morse theory and handle decompositions. Portuguese, with some English and Spanish asides; two short remarks quote ChatGPT. Unfinished. 66 pages. TeX source.

The original build already had a few TeX errors, kept as they are (the style file lacks some operator macros, which were added to the recovered ops.sty). English. 55 pages. TeX source.

    • de Rham and Dolbeault theorems. Own reading notes, May–June 2024, unfinished: sheaves, injective resolutions and derived functors, following Voisin’s book (chapter 4), with Huybrechts for Dolbeault. English. 4 pages. TeX source; the bibliography file is not included, so the citations are unresolved in the PDF.
    • Centro del grupo fundamental de una superficie. Own solution of an exercise (signed Dani), file dated October 2024: if χ(S)<0 then the center of π1(S) is trivial, via the hyperbolic metric and the classification of isometries; it has no title in the PDF and contains paragraphs in English. The bibliography file has a syntax error (a missing comma, as in the original), so one entry is skipped. Spanish. 4 pages. TeX source.
    • Lie groups. Own study notes, March–April 2024, unfinished: classical Lie groups, the Lie algebra of a Lie group and the exponential map, partly a summary of J. M. Lee, Introduction to Smooth Manifolds (a remark in the file says so). The page header reads "Complex manifolds in dimension 1", a leftover. English. 6 pages. TeX source.
    • Notes on math. Own informal study glossary, unfinished and still growing: linear algebra and tensors, sheaves, algebraic topology and homotopy theory, differential and Riemannian geometry, algebraic geometry, complex geometry, Lie groups. It mixes English and Portuguese, quotes some definitions from textbooks, and includes a few passages from conversations with ChatGPT, which the text says so where they occur. Sources last edited August 2025; the course is not recorded in the notes. 159 pages. TeX source.
    • Summary of algebraic topology II. Own study notes, unfinished (TeX of June 2025): homotopy groups and fibrations, Cartesian closed structure and model-categorical language, Postnikov and Whitehead towers, Brown representability and Eilenberg–MacLane spaces, spectral sequences, with questions to self in colour. They draw on lectures and on tom Dieck, Maxim, Burkin and Hatcher; the course is not recorded in the notes. English. 40 pages. TeX source; one cross-reference is unresolved, as in the original.
    • Notes on complex geometry. Own informal study notes, 2025, unfinished: complex and almost complex structures, line bundles and divisors, Riemann–Roch and adjunction for surfaces, ampleness, K3 surfaces, hypercomplex manifolds, with references to lectures of a K3 surfaces course (course not recorded in the notes). Some passages record conversations with ChatGPT and are labelled as such in the text. English. 35 pages. TeX source.
