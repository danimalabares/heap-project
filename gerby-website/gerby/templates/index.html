I’m Daniel González Casanova Azuela (PUC-Rio). The Heap Project is where I keep my notes on courses, seminars, conferences and papers.

Most of the notebook is mathematics. These notes go back to my master’s studies and focus on complex and algebraic geometry. The biology chapters come from my first two years studying biology at UNAM. The physics and computing chapters are new, and much of the writing is AI-generated. The humanities and social sciences chapters are still highly experimental.

The Slides page collects slides from my talks and seminar lectures. Claude is preparing the Seminars section, where you’ll find my seminar notes organized by subject.

It is built with the software of the Stacks Project, so every definition and result has a permanent tag. It is a personal notebook, not a refereed text: it contains errors and unfinished parts.
