## Lessons from today

- plasTeX crashes on `\'\i` inside an argument such as `\emph{...}`. Use the
  plain accented letter. Before pushing new prose, the render can be tested:
  `git archive HEAD | tar -x -C DIR`, then
  `make PYTHON=/usr/local/bin/python3.11 gerby-render` there (about 6 min).
- Railway deploys on push; check with
  `gh api repos/danimalabares/heap-project/deployments`.
- `make NAME.pdf` deletes NAME.aux; cross-chapter refs need
  `make OTHER.aux` first.
- Another session left algebraic-geometry.tex, stanley-reisner.tex and a
  my.bib hunk uncommitted; their ten tags (0HMV to 0HN4) are already in the
  committed registry.
