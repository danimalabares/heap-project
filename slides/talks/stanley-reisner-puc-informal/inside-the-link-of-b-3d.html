What this is

The surface is the link of b: 7 vertices, 15 edges, 10 triangles. The 10 tetrahedra of M that do not touch b fill the inside. The 6 red dashed edges are inside the ball.

Coordinates were found by computer search. It was checked with exact arithmetic that the 10 tetrahedra do not overlap and that their outer surface is the link of b. The shape is one possible straight drawing, not a canonical one, and it is not convex: a sits in a dent. The flat triangle cde (the lid) would close it; the surface goes under the lid through acd, ace, ade. A search for a convex one found none.

The other 10 tetrahedra of M are b joined to the 10 surface triangles. They are the outside. The 7 green rays are the edges from the vertices to b, which is at infinity. The region outside a surface triangle xyz, between its three rays, is the tetrahedron bxyz. The rays all point away from one centre inside the solid; the shape was chosen so that this centre sees every surface triangle, and then the 20 pieces fill 3-space without overlap.
