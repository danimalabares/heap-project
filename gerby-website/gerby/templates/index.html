The Heap Project is the notebook of Daniel González Casanova Azuela (PUC-Rio): notes on courses, seminars, conferences and papers. Most of it is mathematics, mainly complex and algebraic geometry; there are also notes on other subjects, such as biology, chemistry and history.

It is built with the software of the Stacks Project, so every definition and result has a permanent tag. It is a personal notebook, not a refereed text: it contains errors and unfinished parts.
