\section{Introduction: the OpenAI--IMPA collaboration}
\label{section-ai4math-impa-partnership}
These notes begin with the introductory AI4MATH seminar at IMPA on
October 20, 2026. The seminar record below comes from photographs
of slides 3, 4, 5, and 7, not from a complete transcript.

In August 2026 IMPA announced that Centro Pi had been selected for
one of fourteen independent OpenAI-supported projects worldwide,
from over 400 applications. Its project,
\emph{Building Resilient Scientific Research in the Age of Intelligence},
studies how mathematical institutions can use AI efficiently, reliably,
sustainably, and equitably. Topics include infrastructure, computing,
engineering support, training, governance and costs. The six-month
project is led by Daniel Yukimura; the announced team also includes
Francisco Ganacim, Jo\~ao Pedro Ramos, and Cynthia Bortolotto.
OpenAI's support includes funding and API credits. The project plans
to publish an assessment of the costs, infrastructure and responsible
use of AI, potentially applicable to other institutions.

IMPA also advertised up to six master's or doctoral technological
development fellowships, with activities scheduled from September
2026 to March 2027. These include AI-assisted mathematics,
agent development, evaluation tools, formalization with Lean,
literature review, reports and seminars.

\section{Organization and purpose of the seminar}
\label{section-ai4math-format}
Each student works on one open problem for two weeks using an
AI assistant. They then present the mathematics obtained,
including partial and negative results, and discuss how the work
went, including where the assistant helped and where it did not.
The objective is not necessarily to solve the problems but to
generate better mathematical understanding.

The assistant will be developed over time according to students'
experience and seminar feedback. Successful and failed approaches
will be recorded to clarify how AI can and cannot contribute
to mathematical research. The seminar is intended as a place for
open, careful and critical discussion of mathematics, methods
and limitations. It meets every other Tuesday and is open to
everyone at IMPA.

\section{Problems announced on October 20, 2026}
\label{section-ai4math-first-problems}
The following reproduce the mathematical subjects and questions
listed on slide 7. They are \emph{not} claimed results.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The Khavinson--Shapiro conjecture:
Do polynomial Dirichlet data characterize ellipsoids?
(Steinerberger.)
\item Additive energy on grids
$\{0,\ldots,n-1\}^{d}$ and a sharp $L^{4}$ inequality.
(Madrid.)
\item Which radius does the Hardy--Littlewood maximal function
select? (Steinerberger.)
\item Cauchy--Davenport from small subsets:
Is the bound witnessed by subsets of size $O(\sqrt{n})$?
(Tiba.)
\end{enumerate}

\section{Research log}
