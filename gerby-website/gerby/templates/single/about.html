    About

    The Heap Project is the notebook of Daniel González Casanova Azuela (PUC-Rio). It collects notes on courses, seminars, conferences and papers. Most of it is mathematics, mainly complex and algebraic geometry; there are also notes on other subjects, such as biology, chemistry and history.

    Some facts:

    1. It is a personal notebook, not a refereed text. It contains errors and unfinished parts. Corrections are welcome.

    2. The notes are organised in chapters, listed in the table of contents. Every definition and result has a tag, a permanent identifier that does not change when the text is reorganised. See tags explained.

    3. The notes on seminar talks are collected in the seminars notebooks, listed by subject. They are chapters with tags, like the others, but are not in the table of contents.

    4. The notes on conferences, schools and workshops are collected in the conferences notebooks, listed by subject, in the same way.

    5. The slides of talks and seminar lectures are kept separately. They are not chapters and have no tags.

    6. The course projects preserve the original documents I wrote at IMPA. Each entry links to the corresponding notebook sections.

    7. Parts of the notes and slides were written or edited with AI assistants. The commit history records which changes.

    8. The sources are public, in the repository danimalabares/heap-project.
